This IQ data field connects your Garmin bike computer to a Forumslader V5/V6 via BLE and shows live telemetry directly on your device.

Quick Guide:
https://github.com/cyberman54/Forumslader-Companion-public/blob/main/Forumslader-Companion%20Quick%20Guide%20EN.pdf

Functions:

- Up to 4 display fields plus 4 independent FIT recording fields
- Available values include Trip Energy, Temperature, Dynamo Power, Generator Gear, Distance/Odometer, Battery Current, Electrical Load, Speed, Battery Level, Day/Tour Distance, Consumed Energy, Estimated Range, and BLE Diagnostics
- Scroll Fields: single-field tap cycling or compact multi-field view
- Battery/charge icon in the data field
- BLE diagnostics: D (disconnects), E (checksum errors), B (bytes/s)
- Battery estimate by Coulomb Count or voltage model
- Automatic wheel circumference calibration after a continuous 20 km ride
- Optional alerts (low battery, short circuit, system interrupt)
- Optional auto trip reset when activity recording starts

Lock to Device:
When enabled, the app reconnects only to the previously paired Forumslader.

Connectivity:
Automatic scan/connect is built in. After disconnect, the app prefers reconnect first and then falls back to active rescan with backoff.

Compatibility:
Forumslader V5 (with BLE) and V6 are supported.
V6 requires firmware v2.05.2023 or newer:
https://www.forumslader.de/forumslader-pro/technische-details-forumslader-pro/bluetooth-v6

IMPORTANT:
Do not connect the Forumslader to a smartphone app at the same time.
